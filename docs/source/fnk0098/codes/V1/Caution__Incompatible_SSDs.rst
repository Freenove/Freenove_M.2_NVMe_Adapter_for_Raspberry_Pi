##############################################################################
Caution: Incompatible SSDs
##############################################################################

The recognition and reading/writing of the NVME SSD are handled by the drivers of the Raspberry Pi 5. The Freenove M.2 NVMe Adapter for Raspberry Pi (hereinafter referred to as the Adapter) serves to connect and power the SSD. If you find that your SSD is not recognized or readable/writeable by the Pi 5, please try to find the drivers suitable for your SSD and install them on the Raspberry Pi, or replace the SSD, or purchase our Adapter kit that comes with an SSD.
